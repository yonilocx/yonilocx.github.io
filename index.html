YONI LOCX

LIFE | ሕይወት

“LIFE | ሕይወት” explores two sides of the same world: the street life and the fancy life. The street side is about survival, hustling, pressure, loyalty, risks, setbacks, and chasing dreams from the bottom. The fancy side is the other end of the journey: money, luxury, freedom, and good times. The song captures the contrast between struggle and comfort, survival and success, the streets and the high life. Two lifestyles.
