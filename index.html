YONI LOCX

LIFE | ሕይወት

“LIFE | ሕይወት” explores two sides of the same world: the street life and the fancy life.

The street side is about survival, hustling, pressure, loyalty, risks, setbacks, and chasing dreams from the bottom. It’s the struggle, hunger, and reality behind the lifestyle.

The fancy side is the other end of the journey: money, expensive clothes, nice cars, beautiful girls, luxury, freedom, and good times.

The song captures the contrast between struggle and comfort, survival and success, the streets and the high life.

Two lifestyles. One LIFE | ሕይወት.

Biography
